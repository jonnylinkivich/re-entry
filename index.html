RE-ENTRY

Cinder is open. Eight worlds sit far apart across empty space — no gravity, in space or in the caverns. Dock Hab-7 to spend banked credits on guns, engines, tanks, and salvage. Dive the caverns, haul ore, and beat the planet boss for the key to the next world. Fuel is scarce. Hold Shift to raise shields — they drain energy. Run dry and the rescue beam brings you home with half the cargo.

  • A D turn · W thrust forward · S reverse
  • Space / click — shoot. Click a nearby planet to dive.
  • Shift — shields (energy) · E re-enter / launch / dock
  • R — rescue beam when stranded · M or the speaker chip — mute
  • Scroll to zoom. Radar marks planets and the Hab-7 diamond.
  • On a phone or with the on-screen pad: drag — up thrust, L/R turn, down reverse. The center rests, and diagonals ease off together. Fire on the right.
  • Esc — pause / close shop
